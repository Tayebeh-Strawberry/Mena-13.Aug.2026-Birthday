# MENA BIRTHDAY MISSION — CREATOR GUIDE

IMPORTANT:
This guide is for editing the existing Mena Birthday Mission HTML.

The final experience must be a personal interactive birthday mission for Mena.
It must NOT feel like a normal photo slideshow.

Keep these files together in the same main folder:

Mena_Birthday_Mission.html
music.mp3
myvoice.mp3
photos/

1. MUSIC — IMPORTANT

---

Use:

```
music.mp3
```

The Arabic Happy Birthday music must:

* Start when Mena clicks "START MISSION →".
* Continue playing as soft background music throughout the whole mission.
* Do NOT restart the music when moving from one slide/level to another.
* Keep the music gentle and elegant, approximately 15–20% volume.
* The music should continue through all levels.
* Near the final "Asheeeeghetaam" moment, gradually fade the music down.
* Play myvoice.mp3 clearly.
* After my voice finishes, gradually bring the birthday music back.
* The final screen should still have the birthday music softly playing.

IMPORTANT:
The music should begin only after the user clicks START MISSION because browsers may block automatic audio before user interaction.
